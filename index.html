FAQ

GOT A QUESTION?

What programs are available?

There are three programs: Junior Youth Spiritual Empowerment Program, Mernda Neighbourhood Football Club and Mernda Children's classes.

What ages can participate?

Mernda Children's classes are for ages 4–9. The Junior Youth Spiritual Empowerment Program and Mernda Neighbourhood Football Club are for ages 10–15.

When is the Junior Youth program?

Every Tuesday from 4:00 PM to 5:30 PM at Mernda Jindi.

When is football?

Mernda Neighbourhood Football Club is every Saturday from 3:00 PM to 4:30 PM at Parkedge Park Playground.

When are the Children's classes?

Mernda Children's classes are every Tuesday from 4:00 PM to 5:30 PM at Mernda Jindi.

How can I contact the community?

You can call the community on +61 402 523 161.
